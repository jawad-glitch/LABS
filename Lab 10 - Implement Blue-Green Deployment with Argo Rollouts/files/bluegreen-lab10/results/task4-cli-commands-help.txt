== promote
Promote a rollout

Promotes a rollout paused at a canary step, or a paused blue-green pre-promotion.
== undo
Rollback to the previous rollout.

Usage:
== status
Watch rollout until it finishes or the timeout is exceeded. Returns success if
the rollout is healthy upon completion and an error otherwise.
