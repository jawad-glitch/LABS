# CDM Concept Examples

## Illustrative trade information
A trade record may need to represent:
- Trade identifier
- Parties and counterparties
- Product definition
- Economic terms
- Execution and trade dates
- Lifecycle events

## Financial product areas
CDM model coverage includes financial product and transaction concepts. Consult the checked-out Rosetta model and official documentation for the exact supported product types and model version.

## Lifecycle examples
- Execution
- Confirmation
- Allocation
- Settlement
- Payments and other lifecycle events

These are conceptual examples, not validated CDM Rosetta model instances.
