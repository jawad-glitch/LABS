# FINOS (Fintech Open Source Foundation)

## Mission
FINOS is a nonprofit foundation supporting open-source collaboration in financial services.

## Goals
- Encourage collaboration and innovation in financial technology.
- Develop and promote open standards.
- Support a community of developers, technologists, and financial-services professionals.

## Focus areas
- Desktop interoperability, including FDC3.
- Shared financial data models, including CDM.
- Developer tooling and infrastructure.
- Open collaboration and governance.

Project status, governance, and adoption should be verified against each project's current official documentation.
