# Governance Charter

## Model
This project uses hybrid governance: open community participation and accountable
maintainers.

## Contributors
Anyone may report issues, propose changes, submit pull requests, and comment on RFCs.

## Maintainers
Maintainers review contributions, protect project quality, and manage releases.
Maintainer appointments require documented contribution history and community review.

## Technical Steering Committee
The TSC handles strategic direction, governance disputes, and unresolved RFCs.
Decisions and meeting records are documented publicly.

## Decision process
Routine changes are reviewed by maintainers. Significant changes use the RFC process.
The project seeks consensus first. If consensus cannot be reached, the TSC records
the decision and its reasoning.

## Transparency
RFCs, decisions, meeting notes, and governance changes are recorded in the repository.
