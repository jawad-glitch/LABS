NETWORK CONNECTIVITY DIAGNOSIS REPORT
=====================================

ISSUE SUMMARY:
- Frontend cannot connect to backend service
- Test client cannot reach backend service on port 8080
- Connections are being dropped by network policy

ROOT CAUSE ANALYSIS:
1. Network Policy 'backend-deny-all' is blocking all ingress traffic to backend pods
2. The policy has empty ingress rules, effectively denying all incoming connections
3. Backend pods are correctly labeled and targeted by the restrictive policy

EVIDENCE FROM MONITORING:
- Cilium monitor shows policy drops
- Hubble observe confirms DROPPED verdicts for flows to backend
- No successful TCP connections to port 8080 on backend service

RECOMMENDED SOLUTION:
- Modify network policy to allow specific ingress traffic
- Add rules to permit frontend-to-backend communication
- Verify DNS resolution is working correctly
