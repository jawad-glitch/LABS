# Consensus Mechanisms in Distributed Ledgers

## Proof of Work (PoW):
- Miners compete to solve cryptographic puzzles
- First to solve gets to add the next block
- Energy-intensive but highly secure
- Used by Bitcoin

## Proof of Stake (PoS):
- Validators chosen based on stake ownership
- More energy-efficient than PoW
- Risk of centralization with large stakeholders
- Used by Ethereum 2.0

## Practical Byzantine Fault Tolerance (PBFT):
- Designed for permissioned networks
- Can tolerate up to 1/3 malicious nodes
- Fast finality and high throughput
- Used by Hyperledger Fabric

## Raft Consensus:
- Leader-based consensus algorithm
- Simpler than PBFT for crash-fault tolerance
- Used in permissioned blockchain networks
- Provides strong consistency guarantees
