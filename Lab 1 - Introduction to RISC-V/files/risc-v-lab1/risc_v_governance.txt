RISC-V Governance and Community
===============================

RISC-V International:
- Maintains and develops the RISC-V ISA standards.
- Supports technical collaboration and standardization.
- Has members from industry, academia, and the wider community.

Technical Work:
- ISA specifications and extensions.
- Privileged architecture.
- Software and toolchain support.
- Security and platform standards.
- Industry working groups.

Community Participation:
- Review public specifications.
- Participate in technical discussions.
- Contribute to open-source repositories.
- Attend community events and conferences.
- Follow project contribution and governance processes.
