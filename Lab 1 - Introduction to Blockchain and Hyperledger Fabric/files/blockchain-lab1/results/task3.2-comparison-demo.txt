=== Hyperledger Fabric vs Ethereum Comparison Demo ===

1. NETWORK PARTICIPATION:
Fabric: Permissioned - Requires invitation and certificates
Ethereum: Permissionless - Anyone can join with wallet

2. TRANSACTION PRIVACY:
Fabric: Private channels, confidential transactions
Ethereum: All transactions publicly visible on blockchain

3. CONSENSUS DEMONSTRATION:
Fabric Raft Consensus Simulation:
- Leader election among orderer nodes
- Log replication for consistency
- Fast finality (< 1 second)

Ethereum PoW Simulation:
- Miners compete to solve cryptographic puzzle
- Winner adds block and receives reward
- Average 12-15 second block time

4. SMART CONTRACT EXECUTION:
Fabric: Execute-Order-Validate model
Step 1: Execute chaincode on endorsing peers
Step 2: Order transactions into blocks
Step 3: Validate and commit to all peers

Ethereum: Order-Execute model
Step 1: Order transactions in mempool
Step 2: Execute in sequence on EVM
Step 3: Update global state

5. PERFORMANCE METRICS:
Fabric: 3,500+ TPS, sub-second finality
Ethereum: 15 TPS, 12-15 second finality

=== Use Case Recommendations ===
Choose Fabric for:
- Enterprise blockchain solutions
- Private business networks
- Regulatory compliance requirements
- High-performance applications

Choose Ethereum for:
- Public decentralized applications
- Cryptocurrency and DeFi projects
- Global, permissionless access
- Established ecosystem and tools
