# FINOS Project Adopters and Implementations — Research Notes

## Research method
Use official FINOS project pages, repository documentation, project meeting notes, and published organization case studies to verify adoption.

## FDC3
FDC3 is intended for interoperability between financial desktop applications. Its ecosystem includes desktop platform providers, application vendors, and financial institutions.

Organizations and products sometimes associated with FDC3 discussions include financial institutions and technology providers. Treat any specific adopter list as unverified until confirmed by a current primary source.

## CDM
CDM is developed in collaboration with FINOS and ISDA participants and is intended to standardize financial transaction and lifecycle representations.

## Evidence discipline
Do not treat an organization's membership, participation in a working group, or use of a related product as proof that it has deployed a particular project in production.

No percentage savings or quantified benefits are asserted in this lab because no specific measured case study has been independently verified here.
